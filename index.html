Vuemio

A television guide and launcher for Apple TV.

Vuemio brings live listings together in one guide and opens each programme in the broadcaster's own app. It does not stream or host video, and it collects no personal data.

Vuemio Stream

Companion IPTV media player for Apple TV.

Vuemio Stream is a free companion tvOS player for personal IPTV playlists, featuring EPG catchup, movies, and series metadata powered by The Movie Database (TMDB). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
