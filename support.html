Common questions

A channel opens the wrong programme, or the wrong app

Vuemio opens the broadcaster's own app using a link that occasionally changes at the provider's end. Channel links are updated remotely, so the fix usually arrives on its own within a day. If a channel stays wrong, email us and name the channel.

A channel won't open at all

The broadcaster's app must be installed on your Apple TV, and for most services you need to be signed in there already. Vuemio never asks for your television sign-in details.

Listings look wrong or are missing

Guide data comes from public sources and can occasionally lag. Check that your region is correct under Settings → General → Guide Region.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing

Open Settings → About → Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase. Purchases are also shared with family members if Family Sharing is enabled for the app.

How do I clear my settings?

Settings → General → Reset All Settings to Defaults. This clears favourites, MyList and preferences on this Apple TV. It does not affect your purchase.
