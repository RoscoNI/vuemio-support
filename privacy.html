Vuemio Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

Vuemio is a television guide and launcher for Apple TV. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

Vuemio does not collect, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no server of ours to send it to.

What stays on your Apple TV

Everything you set up in Vuemio is stored only on your device:

  • Favourite channels and the order you put them in
  • MyList saved programmes
  • Recently watched channels
  • Settings — theme, app icon, tile size, region, Top Shelf preferences

You can erase all of it at any time using Reset All Settings inside the app, or by deleting Vuemio from your Apple TV.

Connections the app makes

To show listings and artwork, Vuemio requests data directly from public television guide sources and from broadcasters' own artwork servers. These requests go straight from your Apple TV to those providers — they do not pass through us. As with any internet request, the provider receiving it can see ordinary connection information such as your IP address. All of these requests use HTTPS.

Vuemio also downloads a small channel configuration file so channel links can be corrected without an app update. It contains no information about you.

Watching programmes

Vuemio does not stream or host any video. Choosing a programme opens the broadcaster's own Apple TV app, and everything from that point on is governed by that provider's privacy policy, not this one.

Vuemio never asks for, receives or stores passwords or sign-in details for any television service.

Purchases

Vuemio Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. Vuemio receives only whether the purchase is active. We never see your payment details.

Children

Vuemio is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.
